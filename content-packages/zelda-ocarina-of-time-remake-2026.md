## 3. Draft voiceover (about 175–195 words)
"Can you remake one of the most beloved games ever made without losing what made it special?

Nintendo is bringing Ocarina of Time back on Switch 2 — but this isn't being presented as just a resolution boost.

The official overview promises overhauled visuals, fully voiced cutscenes with expanded dialogue, and a symphonic orchestral soundtrack. There's also a new twist on the game's most iconic tool: the Switch 2 microphone can listen while you hum or play melodies for Link's ocarina.

And there's a new Hall of Fallen Heroes, where you can study defeated monsters — or challenge them again.

Those are real, announced features. But the hardest part of a remake isn't adding more detail. It's deciding what should stay untouched.

Ocarina of Time helped define 3D adventure games for a generation. Better controls and richer environments can make the journey easier to revisit, but atmosphere, pacing, and discovery are what people remember.

So here's the question: should a remake preserve the original as closely as possible, or should it take bigger risks to feel new?

The remake launches on Nintendo Switch 2 on November 5, 2026. Which side are you on?"
