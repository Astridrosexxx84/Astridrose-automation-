# Rights and production-effort gate

Purpose: decide how much work a trend deserves without confusing availability, editability, and legal permission.

## Per-item decision states

- **GREEN — permission/licence appears suitable:** original item page and licence checked; intended commercial use, adaptations, attribution and share-alike obligations understood; save evidence and required credit. Still perform a human check.
- **AMBER — permission unclear or restricted:** do not download into the edit or publish. Check official terms, seek permission, or replace with an item whose terms permit the use.
- **EDITORIAL-REVIEW — potentially transformative commentary/criticism:** describe the specific analytical purpose, why this exact excerpt is needed, amount used, and alternatives. This is a jurisdiction-specific legal assessment, not a licence or guarantee. Keep excerpts proportionate; get advice when stakes justify it.
- **RED — no defensible permission or editorial rationale:** reject the item, change the angle, or use licensed/public-domain material or original visuals.

## Important rule

Cropping, mirroring, speeding up, changing colour, adding borders, overlays, captions or audio do **not** by themselves create permission or make a copyrighted recording legally reusable. Never use these edits merely to evade copyright detection or disguise a source. Edits should serve a real editorial, explanatory or accessibility purpose.

## Effort tiers

1. **Quick (under 30 minutes):** clear licence/public domain, enough relevant material, simple story.
2. **Worth extra effort (30–120 minutes):** strong audience signal and a plausible route via permissions, licensed alternatives, original graphics, data, stills, or genuine commentary.
3. **Hold/reselect:** no usable visuals after a bounded search, unclear rights with no permission path, weak research, or production effort outweighs evidence of audience demand.

These are planning estimates, not legal conclusions.

## Required evidence for each asset

- Original item page and direct file URL (if available)
- Creator / rights holder
- Exact licence and licence URL, or written permission record
- Commercial use and adaptation permission
- Attribution text and any share-alike requirements
- Whether the media actually plays/downloads
- Planned use and editorial purpose
- Alternative if rejected
- Final human decision and reviewer/date

## Output policy

This gate is for planning and review, not automated legal clearance. No automatic publishing. No asset is approved solely because its search result includes a licence field.
