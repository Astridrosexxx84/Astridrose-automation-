## Editorial direction (provisional)
Working title: **Earth From Space: 5 Things You Never Notice From the Ground**
1. At night, cities become patterns of light — and the image is a satellite composite, not one camera snapshot.
2. Weather systems become moving shapes when seen across a continent.
3. The horizon reveals a thin atmospheric edge; use real ISS footage if a clean segment is available.
4. Satellite instruments can show information outside ordinary human vision; explain false colour rather than presenting it as natural colour.
5. Repeated observations reveal change over time: water, ice, vegetation or coastlines. Only use a specific example if a verified source and suitable visuals are found.

Avoid implying that all lights represent population, that all visualizations are natural-colour photographs, or that a time-composite is a single moment. Narration must add explanation, not simply describe what is on screen.
