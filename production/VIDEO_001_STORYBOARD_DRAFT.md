# VIDEO_001 — Storyboard v0 (source-led)
Status: draft for assembly planning; **not a publishing approval**.
Working title: Earth From Space: 5 Things You Never Notice From the Ground
Target: 3–4 minutes, 16:9, original narration, restrained sound design, no unlicensed music.

## Editorial rule
Use actual footage or a real scientific visualization for every major beat. Narration must explain the observation and distinguish direct imagery from processed/composite data. No filler footage just to hit runtime. Final scene choices depend on the selected files being checked for reuse and downloaded/previewed.

## Research facts that can anchor narration
- NASA’s night-light composite uses satellite data acquired over multiple days, not a single exposure: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/11157/
- ISS airglow footage shows red and green swaths along Earth’s limb: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/12220/
- Landsat is used to observe surface change, including urban change, water use, agriculture, glaciers and forest health: https://science.nasa.gov/missions/landsat/landsats-global-perspective/
- NASA’s glacial-speed visualization describes satellite imagery/data mapping the speed of flowing ice: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/video-at-glacial-speed/

## Production guardrails
- Do not download the largest files by default; use practical HD versions where quality is adequate.
- Do not use the entire NASA+ 4K ambient video as a substitute for a narrative; use a specifically cleared excerpt only if its media terms allow it.
- No AI-generated visuals unless the inspected source set reveals a real, material gap.
- No AI generation credits spent without checking free-tier availability and getting consent if a spend/limited credit may be involved.
- No YouTube upload until the rendered video has been reviewed for visual continuity, narration clarity, factual accuracy, rights/credits, and audio levels.
