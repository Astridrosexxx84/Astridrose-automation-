# 30-day creator content calendar

A reusable, low-pressure starter calendar for a playful transformation-fantasy creator persona. Treat every item as a draft: review platform rules and your own comfort before posting. Keep paid-content links only where platform rules permit.

## Posting cadence
- Publish one original post on scheduled days; do not mass-post or auto-comment.
- Reply to genuine comments manually and respectfully.
- Track clicks and conversions, not just likes.
- Reuse an idea only after changing the angle; avoid duplicate cross-posting.

## Simple measurement loop
After each post, add a row to metrics.csv. Record what you can actually verify. Leave unknown numbers blank rather than guessing.

## Safety and platform notes
- Check each platform's current rules for adult themes, AI-generated content, promotion, and automation.
- Do not imply a medical procedure is risk-free or encourage anyone to make medical decisions for a fictional storyline.
- Avoid unsolicited DMs, repetitive comments, engagement bait, and automated replies.
- Disclose affiliate relationships if you later use affiliate links.
